# OOGA Team Contract
### Team Name: Good Piggies
### Georgiy Zemlevskiy, Michael Li, Paulina Vvedenskaya, Iurii Beliaev, Mason Wu, Xuye Chen, Emilio Garcia


## As a project team, we promise to:
 * respect each other's ideas and work
 * communicate with the team regularly to keep everyone in the loop about the project
 * encourage participation and attempt to help whenever possible


## Team Procedures
 * when and how often to meet (at least once a week)
	* Once a week on Sundays, preferably in person.
	* Tentative time: 2:00 PM
 * preferred channel of communication (e.g., Zoom, e-mail, WhatsApp, Slack, etc.)
	* Text & Discord
 * preferred GIT workflow
	* Dev & main persistent branches. Team members are free to branch off of dev and merge feature branches. Merges from dev into main must go through merge requests approved by another team members. 
 

### Meetings 
 * how to communicate action items made during the meeting
    * We will write a short and minimal meeting notes summary that will be sent out in our discord server. We will also summarize and assign next stage objectives to each member so they are clear about what they are working on.

 * policy for a team member to give advance notice of missing a team meeting and work required to make up for it 
    * team members will give 24 hour notice before missing meetings
    * team members will go over the meeting notes to be kept in the loop with the discussion
 * policy for handling disagreements, major decisions, or priorities during the meeting
    * disagreements will be handled using respect and kindness. 
    * major decisions including major design change or large merge requests will be reviewed by multiple members during meeting
    * priorities will be set based on core feature requirements based on consensus.


### Communications
 * decision-making policy (e.g., by consensus? by majority vote?)
	* We will go for a majority vote after a discussion
 * response policy (e.g., respond within 24 hours)
	* Respond in the discord within 24 hours, and in the text groupchat within 12.
 * policy for keeping everyone up-to-date on major decisions, changes, or updates 
	* Everyone will strive to keep the team updated with decision changes. Status updates every 2-3 days. Also please let other members know when updates happen so they are notified of changes.

### Commitments
 * what does it mean to work to the "best of your ability"
	* Being on time to meetings and active communication
	* Finish assigned issues/tasks in a timely manner
 * how to set internal deadlines
	* Each person will set a deadline for each of their responsibilities for the sprint. The team as a whole will agree on deadlines for broader tasks.
    * how to provide commitments others on the team can rely on
 * Set realistic deadlines for yourself, and if you feel that you cannot meet a deadline, notify the team as soon as possible.
 * policy for resolving failure to meet commitments
	* Initial team discussion to figure out why that happened and how others can help. If it doesn’t help - escalate to the teaching team (Prof. Duvall, TAs)

### Helping each other
 * best ways to and when to ask for help
	* Talk to each other via text or the discord. We will create smaller subgroups for each task later on once we distribute the work more, and teammates will be encouraged to reach out to the people in their subgroup.
 * responses to team member asking for help
	* Teammates will be open minded and kind when others are asking for help. We all understand that everyone has slightly different CS backgrounds and will be willing to work with each other
 * how to support the team priorities
	* We all prioritize the Oogasalad project quite highly, but recognize that there may be other commitments/midterms that are more important on a week-to-week basis, but overall, we will strive to produce quality work/code. 
 * how to suggest design changes for specific pieces of code
	* During our weekly meeting, anyone should feel free to bring up design changes, but we can also text each other in the discord or iMessage chat. 
	* If the team members concerned with the code agree that a change should be made, an issue should be added so that this is not forgotten.
 

## If someone on our team breaks one or more of these agreements, the team may:
 * have a team meeting and ask the person to follow this agreement
 * report the issue in the Team Sprint Report
 * escalate to the teaching team and Prof. Duvall if the issue is not resolved
